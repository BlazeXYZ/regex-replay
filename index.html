    Growth probe

    How does the work grow as the input gets longer? The probe repeats a piece of text 1, 2, 3… times, adds an ending, and counts the steps for each length.

    What's under the hood

    A regex parser and backtracking matcher written from scratch for this page. It follows JavaScript's matching rules and records every decision it makes, and each run is checked against your browser's own RegExp.

    Supported syntax

    Literals and escapes, ., classes like [^a-z\d], ^ $ \b \B, capturing, non-capturing and named groups, |, greedy and lazy * + ? {n,m}, lookahead (?= ) (?! ), and backreferences \1 \k<name>. Flags g, i, m, s. Lookbehind and Unicode mode are not supported yet.

    Keyboard

    Space play or pause, ← → step, Shift + arrows jump between backtracks, Home End jump to the ends.
